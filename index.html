FROM IDEA TO DELIVERY

Ordering is a
conversation.

Choose from thirteen wall pieces or bring a custom idea. Tell Tisho the design, colour where applicable, and size; the team will quote directly in the conversation.

  1. 01Pick a piece

    Explore HOME, Home Heart, Proudly Kenyan, Nairobi, Get Rich or Die Tryin and Nipsey designs—or start with an idea of your own.

  2. 02DM / WhatsApp

    Message @tisho.apparel or WhatsApp 0746 099 135 with the piece name, preferred colour where shown, and your size from S, M, L, XL or XXL.

  3. 03Get your quote

    Tisho confirms the price directly in the conversation. The site never publishes or estimates a price.

  4. 04Design & print

    For custom work, bring the idea and discuss the design with Tisho. The Nairobi team then designs and prints your approved order.

  5. 05Packaging & delivery

    Your order is prepared in a sleek black gift box lined with grey tissue paper, as shown in the Nairobi City feature.

FAMILY-RUN · NAIROBI CBD

Printed here.
Worn everywhere.

Luxury | Premium Streetwear | Kenyan Made — Graphic Tees & Hoodies

Tisho Apparel is a family-run apparel printing and design company in Nairobi CBD, Kenya. Its catalogue moves between place-inspired graphics, HOME and Home Heart colourways, bold statement tees, and custom client work.

Think it, we design it and print it on your T-shirt. Choose a ready-made graphic tee or hoodie, or bring a custom idea to the Nairobi team. Every garment is available in S, M, L, XL and XXL, with orders handled directly by DM or WhatsApp 0746 099 135.

There are no published prices: Tisho quotes in the conversation. Orders are finished in a sleek black gift box lined with grey tissue paper.
